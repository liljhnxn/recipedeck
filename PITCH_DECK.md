### Slide 2: The Problem (Web2 Cooking is Broken)
* **Ad Bloat & Tracking**: Modern cooking blogs have become unreadable walls of auto-playing video ads, pop-ups, and trackers.
* **SEO Life Stories**: Searching for a quick pasta dish requires scrolling past 2,000 words of algorithmic fluff.
* **Fragile Availability**: Family recipes and classic blog posts disappear when servers shut down or domain names expire.
* **Centralized Monopolies**: Big food platforms own and monetize user contributions without creator sovereignty.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution (RecipeDeck)
* **100% On-Chain Culinary Commons**: Recipe titles, stories, categories, ingredients, instructions, and likes live forever on Botchain.
* **Ad-Free & Respectful**: Zero trackers, zero banners, zero algorithm-driven feed manipulation.
* **No Speculation, Zero Barriers**: No tokens, no NFTs, and no paywalls. Pure utility and culture.
* **Creator-Owned**: Only the author's cryptographic signature can edit or remove a recipe.

---

### Slide 4: Product Experience & Aesthetics
* **Artisanal Food Magazine Design**: Warm terracotta orange, linen cream paper, charcoal serif typography, and golden honey accents.
* **Interactive Kitchen Checklist**: Tappable ingredient checkmarks so cooks can check off items as they prep.
* **Live Card Preview**: Authors preview their exact recipe card before submitting their transaction to the blockchain.
* **Instant Category Filters & Search**: Real-time filtering across Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Dessert, Vegetarian, Bakery, and Beverages.

---

### Slide 5: Technical Highlights
* **Strict Single Page**: `app/page.tsx` built with Next.js App Router, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, and wagmi v2 / viem.
* **Strict Single Contract**: `RecipeDeck.sol` deployed and verified on Botchain Testnet (Chain ID 968).
* **Zero Backend or Central DB**: True peer-to-peer dApp reading directly from RPC `https://rpc.bohr.life`.
* **On-Chain Sybil Protection**: Smart contract prevents duplicate likes from the same wallet address.
* **100% Test Coverage**: 11 automated unit tests verifying publishing, liking, unliking, and removal.
